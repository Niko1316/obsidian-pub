On September 24, 2026, crypto exchange Bitget lost nearly $388 million, and only about $1.1 million has been frozen since, according to its CEO.

Here is the detail most headlines skip: no private keys were compromised and the cold wallets were untouched. The attackers forged withdrawal orders by exploiting a flaw in a third-party security product.

The weak link was not bitcoin, it was the trust placed in a chain of intermediaries.

BITCOIN DECODED, the manual your banker will never give you, unpacks exactly that: what holding your own bitcoin actually means, in plain language and with no promise of profit.
