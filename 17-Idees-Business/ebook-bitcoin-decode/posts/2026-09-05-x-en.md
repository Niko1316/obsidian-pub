On September 15, the US Senate votes on the CLARITY Act, the bill that finally draws the line: SEC for tokens that behave like securities, CFTC for commodities like Bitcoin.

If the Senate stalls, the CFTC and the SEC publish their own rules days later. Either way, the legal gray zone closes in 2026.

For 17 years, institutions waited for clear rules before committing. That wait is ending, and it will shape the next decade of crypto.

The less you understand Bitcoin, the more headlines scare you. The more you understand it, the more you see the pattern.
